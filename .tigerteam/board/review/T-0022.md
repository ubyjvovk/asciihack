## Goal
In the PM's 200×100 capture of ortho v2 the hero stood next to the room's
south wall and the wall block, painted later in painter's order, covered
most of the hero figure. Classic isometric problem; classic fix: cut away
walls that stand between the camera and the hero, and never let the hero
be fully hidden.

## Context
- `src/render/ortho.ts` (v2, T-0021: wall pass in painter's order, figures
  drawn with their tile), `docs/render.md` "Ortho renderer".
- **Cutaway.** A wall cell is "in front of the hero" when its `(x + y)` is
  greater than the hero's and it is within 2 cells of the hero in both
  axes (`|dx| ≤ 2 && |dy| ≤ 2`). Paint such blocks at 0.35 brightness
  (faces and top) — a translucent-looking ghost block — and do **not**
  clear `overlayCh` where they overlap a figure that was already drawn:
  the figure's letters stay, dimmed to 0.7. Apply the same rule to walls
  in front of any monster within 2 cells of the hero, so a jackal behind
  a wall corner is still visible.
- **Hero always on top.** After the wall pass, re-stamp the hero figure's
  cells: any cell of the hero figure that a later wall overwrote gets the
  hero letter back at 0.6 brightness (an x-ray silhouette), so the hero is
  never invisible even when the cutaway rule does not apply.
- Regenerate `tests/ortho-zoom-golden.txt` (its room has the hero one cell
  from a wall, so the golden will change).
