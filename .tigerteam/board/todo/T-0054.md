## Goal
Two things the PM measured after T-0052 and T-0053 landed. Both are decided;
apply the numbers.

## Context
- **The sources are vendored in-repo at `vendor/afterburn/` (read-only).**
  `~/afterburn` is not visible inside the worker container.
- Read `docs/gpu-thirdperson.md` (its "Fog" section, which this ticket
  supersedes), `docs/gpu-dungeon.md` and `docs/gpu.md` §3.3.

### 1. The third-person view is too dark
T-0052 measured `e^(−0.10 · 10.7) ≈ 34 %` fog survival at the default
third-person distance and concluded that was comfortable. Shooting it proved
otherwise — mean luminance over the frame, same pose, same mood:

| view | mean |
|---|---|
| fps | 54.0 |
| ortho | 28.8 |
| **third** | **8.0** |

34 % survival *plus* inverse-square falloff over ~10 cells is too much loss.
`web/src/gpu/ortho.ts` already owns the per-view fog knobs
(`FPS_FOG_DENSITY = 0.10`, `ORTHO_FOG_DENSITY = 0.01`) and
`moodFogDensityForView`, which currently maps `'third'` → `'fps'`. Add
**`THIRD_FOG_DENSITY = 0.04`** (65 % survival at 10.7 cells) and map
`'third'` to it. Scale the mood's own density by the same ratio the ortho
path uses, so `deep_dark` stays proportionally heavier.

### 2. Dry stone lost its definition
T-0053 correctly made stone dry, but wetness had been doing double duty: it
darkened albedo *and* its sheen picked out flagstone edges. Without it the
floor reads as a flat tan expanse. Measured on the amber ASCII output, same
pose, before → after T-0053:

| | black | mean | p95 | **levels** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| wet stone | 77.4 % | 10.4 | 62 | **148** |
| dry stone | 74.7 % | 11.4 | 66 | **134** |

Brightness is fine — it is the **14 lost levels** that matter, because
distinct levels are exactly what the ASCII quantiser spends. Restore the
variation in the geometry rather than by making things wet again: in
`dungeon.ts`, raise the **per-box colour jitter on floor and wall stone from
±4 % to ±8 %**. `vendor/afterburn/docs/ART_BIBLE.md` §2 asks for ±3–6 %, but
that is for a surface with a wet sheen on it; say in the docs that we went
above their range deliberately, and why.
