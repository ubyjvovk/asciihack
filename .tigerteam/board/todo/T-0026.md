## Goal
Monsters and items are currently generic ellipses nearly as tall as a
wall. Draw them from NetHack's own 16×16 tile art instead: the billboard
shows the sprite's letter only where the tile has a pixel, in that pixel's
colour, so a jackal is a jackal-shaped cluster of `d`s and a potion a
small bottle of `!`s; and size them by NetHack's monster size class so a
newt is knee-high and a dragon fills the corridor. Letter figures remain
the fallback for anything without a tile.

## Context
- `Sprite.height` / `Sprite.tile` and the `Tile` type: `src/model/types.ts`
  (PM-owned). Tiles + names: `src/render/tiles.ts`, `docs/tiles.md`
  (T-0025). **The tables arrive as a separate bridge line**
  `{"t":"tables", monsters, objects}` right after `init_nhwindows`
  (`TablesMsg` in `src/engine/protocol.ts`, PM-owned; see
  `docs/bridge.md` "hello and tables"): `monsters[idx]` gives `name`,
  `male`/`female`, `letter`, `size` (MZ_*: 0 tiny, 1 small, 2 medium,
  3 large, 4 huge, 7 gigantic), `objects[idx]` gives `name`/`descr`/`cls`;
  `GlyphInfo.idx` is that index for `cls` mon/pet/… and obj; `MG_FEMALE`
  in `flags` selects the female tile. First step of this ticket: make
  `NethackSession` store that message as `session.tables: TablesMsg |
  null` (a `case 'tables'` in its message switch; one test in
  `tests/engine-session.test.ts`: after handling a tables line,
  `session.tables.monsters[0].name` is set) and document it in
  `docs/engine.md`.
- `src/ui/view3d.ts` `spritesFromMap`: attach `height` and `tile`. Heights
  in cells: tiny 0.3, small 0.5, medium 0.7, large 0.9, huge 1.1,
  gigantic 1.3 (taller than the ceiling gets clipped, that is fine);
  objects 0.35, except `boulder` 0.7 and `chest`/`large box` 0.5;
  corpses (`cls` body) 0.3. Tile: `monsterTile(name, female)` for monster
  classes (statues too), `objectTile(name, descr)` for obj/body. Names come
  from `session.tables` (above); without tables, sprites get no tile.
- **Raycaster** (`src/render/raycast.ts`, sprite pass): billboard width =
  `height` (tiles are square; the cell aspect correction already applies),
  bottom on the floor at the sprite's distance, height `height` cells.
  For each screen cell of the billboard compute `(u, v)` in 0..1, sample
  the tile pixel `floor(u·16), floor(v·16)`; skip transparent pixels
  (palette index 0); otherwise write `overlayCh = sprite letter` and
  `overlayRgb = palette colour / 255` (linear-ish is fine) times fog and
  a slight vertical shading (`0.85 + 0.15·(1 − v)`). Depth test per cell
  as today. When the billboard is shorter than 2 rows or narrower than 2
  columns, draw the single letter in the sprite's colour (today's far
  rule). Sprites without a tile keep the ellipse figure at the given
  `height` (no longer a fixed 0.9).
- **Ortho** (`src/render/ortho.ts`): same sampling on a billboard
  `round(height · 3.5k / 0.9)` rows tall and as many columns wide as keeps
  it square on screen (×2 for the cell aspect), feet at the tile centre
  row; transparent pixels leave the floor visible; no rim needed.
- Goldens: add a jackal (with its real tile from `tiles.json`) and a
  potion to the textured raycaster golden and the ortho zoom golden and
  regenerate. Keep the ellipse-figure tests but at explicit heights.
- Performance: sampling is per billboard cell, no allocation; the 200×60
  timing tests must stay under 8 ms with three tiled sprites in view.
