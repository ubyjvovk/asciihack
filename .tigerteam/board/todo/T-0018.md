## Goal
In the first real session the user could not tell which way they were
facing. Add three cheap, always-visible cues: a compass ribbon along the
top of the first-person viewport (AsciiCity's BEARING idea), the hero drawn
as a facing arrow on the minimap, and a small fixed rose in the ortho view
(whose north is up-right on screen).

## Context
- `docs/ui.md` (mode interface, `paintViewport(grid, rect)`, minimap),
  `src/ui/modes/fps.ts` (owns `facing` and the interpolated `yaw`),
  `src/ui/minimap.ts`, `src/ui/modes/ortho.ts`, `src/ui/view3d.ts`
  (`FACINGS`), `src/ui/grid.ts` (`putText`). Coordinates: `docs/architecture.md`
  §7 (yaw 0 = N, +π/2 = E, clockwise).
- **Compass ribbon** (`src/ui/compass.ts`, pure: `paintCompass(grid, rect,
  yawRad)`): drawn on the first row of the viewport rect, centred, 41
  columns wide, over the rendered scene. Headings `N NE E SE S SW W NW`
  (angles 0°, 45°, …) are placed at column `centre + round((Δ / 35°) · 20)`
  where `Δ` is the signed angular difference heading − yaw normalised to
  (−180°, 180°]; only headings with |Δ| ≤ 35° are shown (that matches the
  70° field of view: what you see is what the ribbon spans); a `·` tick
  every 15° in between; the centre column shows `|` under the label. The
  nearest heading is drawn bright white, others dim grey (`[120,120,120]`),
  ticks darker; background black so it reads over any scene. It uses the
  interpolated yaw so it slides during a turn.
- **Minimap facing arrow** (`src/ui/minimap.ts`): `paintMinimap` takes an
  optional `facing` (the `Facing` from `FACINGS`); when given, the hero
  cell prints an arrow instead of `@`: `↑ ↗ → ↘ ↓ ↙ ← ↖` for N…NW
  (U+2191, U+2197, U+2192, U+2198, U+2193, U+2199, U+2190, U+2196; all
  BMP, one cell wide — fine for `ScreenCell.ch`), inverse-video as today.
  Without `facing` (ortho, classic) it stays `@`.
- **Ortho rose**: a fixed 5×3 legend at the top-left of the ortho
  viewport, dim grey with the `+` bright:
  ```
  W   N
    +
  S   E
  ```
  because in the locked projection north is up-right (cell (x, y−1) →
  screen (sx+2, sy−1)), east down-right, south down-left, west up-left.
- Keep it out of the classic mode. Nothing here touches the renderers.
