Then re-measure with `python3 scripts/shot-stats.py`. **Target for the raw
`?stack=dungeon&q=high&mood=torchlit` frame at 640×360, hero in the lit

light measured `mean 92.6 / black 5.2 %`; the legacy path measured

If rows 1–5 alone land inside the band, stop and leave 6–9 for a later pass;
say so in the report rather than pushing every row.
