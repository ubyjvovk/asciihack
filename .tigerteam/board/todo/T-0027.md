## Goal
T-0023 gave the first-person view AsciiCity's readability: dark surfaces
that quantize to sparse glyphs, bright edges drawn at absolute brightness.
The ortho renderer shares `KIND_COLORS` and so inherited the dark bases but
still scales its rims and faces as multiples of them, which makes the
isometric room dim and flat. Give it the same grammar: dark block faces,
bright top rims and corner lines, dark seams, distance fade.

## Context
- `docs/render.md` ("Ortho renderer", and the raycaster's camera/look
  sections from T-0023 for the levels), `src/render/ortho.ts` (v2 + cutaway
  + tile sprites from T-0026).
- Levels (linear, before exposure; the quantizer is unchanged):
  - Wall block faces: base from `KIND_COLORS` (wall 0.14 …) × 0.75 (SE) /
    0.55 (SW) with the brick texture body ±0.03 and mortar at `0.05`
    absolute; the **top face** `0.16` flat (a dark lid).
  - Rims and lines at **absolute** brightness: the top face's upper two
    edges `0.75`, its lower two edges `0.45`, the vertical corner between
    the faces `0.55`, the block's bottom contact line `0.25`.
  - Floor tiles: base from `KIND_COLORS.floor` with the flagstone texture
    (import `floorShade` from `texture.ts`, stones ±20 %), diamond seams
    `0.30` absolute; checker removed (the stones carry the variation);
    corridors `KIND_COLORS.corridor`, no seams.
  - Doorway / open door tiles: floor in the door colour with the two
    post cells at `0.70`.
  - Unexplored lattice unchanged (`0.05`); fog `exp(−0.06·dist)` applied to
    faces and floor but **not** to rims within 3 cells of the hero (near
    edges stay crisp), then normally beyond.
  - Cutaway ghost blocks keep their 0.35 factor applied to these levels.
- Regenerate both ortho goldens; add a jackal sprite to the zoom golden if
  T-0026 has not already.
