## Goal
Walking the hero along the southern wall of a room hides him behind it: the
third-person camera sits south of the hero at a 42° pitch, so that wall is
between the camera and the player. The user wants the classic isometric
behaviour — *"the diablo thing where we see through that wall when player is
near"*.

## Context
- **The sources are vendored in-repo at `vendor/afterburn/` (read-only).**
  `~/afterburn` is not visible inside the worker container.
- **This is the follow-up `docs/gpu-ortho.md` already names.** That file's
  section *"What (a) does not deliver — and the follow-up"* explains why the
  current ghost-mesh cutaway cannot fix this: the GPU dungeon is **one merged
  geometry per chunk**, so individual wall cells cannot be hidden the way the
  legacy `InstancedMesh` hides them. The ghost mesh is an overlay drawn *on
  top of* the still-opaque wall. Read that section first — it also records
  that the intended fix is **option (b), a clip volume in the material**.
  This ticket is option (b).
- **The design, decided.** Add a hero cutout to `createVoxelMaterial`, driven
  by uniforms the render loop writes each frame:
  - `cutoutCenter` (vec3, the hero's world position), `cutoutRadius`
    (default **2.5** cells), `cutoutFade` (**0.8** cells), `cutoutForward`
    (vec3, the horizontal camera→hero direction) and `cutoutEnabled` (0/1).
  - A fragment is cut when **all** of:
    1. it is between the camera and the hero —
       `dot(fragWorld − cutoutCenter, cutoutForward) < 0`;
    2. its horizontal distance to `cutoutCenter` is under `cutoutRadius`;
    3. its world **Y is above the floor** (`> 0.05`) — never cut the floor
       out from under the hero, only what stands up in the way.
  - Fade with **screen-door dithering**, not alpha blending: compare a
    per-pixel dither pattern against the 0→1 ramp across `cutoutFade` and
    `discard`. Alpha transparency would pull these surfaces out of the
    opaque pass and corrupt the G-buffer that SSGI and SSR read; a discard
    keeps the material opaque and simply makes the wall genuinely absent for
    those rays, which is what we want.
- Put the pure geometry in `web/src/gpu/cutout.ts`:
  `cutoutForwardFor(cameraPos, heroPos)` (normalised horizontal direction)
  and `isCutCell(cellX, cellY, hero, forward, radius)` — the same predicate
  in cell terms, so it is testable in node without a renderer and so the
  docs can state the rule exactly.
- **Enable in `third` and `ortho`, disable in `fps`** (in first person the
  camera *is* the hero, so nothing is ever between them).
- Leave the existing ghost-mesh cutaway in place for now; it is harmless and
  removing it is a separate cleanup. Say in the report whether it is now
  redundant.
