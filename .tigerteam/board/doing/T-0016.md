## Goal
In the PM's playtest a flat-shaded wall three cells away fills the whole
viewport with one repeated glyph, so the first-person view reads as a
blank block instead of a dungeon. Give surfaces procedural detail so
depth and structure are visible in ASCII: brick texture on walls, a
perspective grid on floors, frames around doorways, light edge lines on
wall tops and corners. All procedural, all deterministic, all pure.

## Context
- `docs/architecture.md` §5.2 and `docs/render.md`; the renderer is
  `src/render/raycast.ts` (`renderFirstPerson`, `KIND_COLORS`), tests and
  golden in `tests/raycast.test.ts` / `tests/raycast-golden.txt`. Keep
  the existing behaviour reachable: `RaycastOptions.detail?: boolean`
  (default **true**); the existing golden is regenerated with
  `detail: false` and must stay byte-identical; a second golden
  `tests/raycast-textured-golden.txt` covers the default.
- Put the pure pattern functions in `src/render/texture.ts` and unit-test
  them there; `raycast.ts` only calls them.
- **Wall texture** — for a wall hit compute `u` (fraction along the face:
  `posY + perp·rdy` for a vertical face, `posX + perp·rdx` for a
  horizontal one, take the fractional part) and, per screen row, `v`
  (0 at the wall top, 1 at the bottom). `brickShade(u, v, seed)`: brick
  rows 0.25 tall, bricks 0.5 wide, every other row offset by 0.25;
  mortar lines 0.05 wide at 0.65; brick body at 1.0 ± 0.08 from a hash
  of (brick row, brick column, seed) where `seed` is the map cell index
  (`y·80 + x`) so bricks are stable frame to frame. Multiply the wall
  colour by it (after the N/S vs E/W face factor, before fog).
- **Doors** — `door_closed`: `plankShade(u)` = vertical planks 0.2 wide
  alternating 1.0 / 0.82 with a 0.05 dark seam, plus a frame: the outer
  0.12 of the face (`u < 0.12 || u > 0.88`) at the wall colour × 1.1.
  `doorway` / `door_open` cells stay passable, but the ray treats the
  outer 0.12 of the cell's width (the posts) as solid wall-coloured
  frame so a doorway reads as an opening in a wall rather than a gap
  in the floor colour. Bars: `barsShade(u)` = 0.2-wide bars at 1.0
  separated by 0.3 at 0.25.
- **Floor grid** — in the floor pass, `gridShade(fX, fY)`: 0.7 when the
  sample is within 0.05 of a cell edge in either axis, else 1.0; apply
  to `floor`, `ice`, `stairs_*`, `altar`, `throne` cells only (not
  corridors, water, lava). Stairs get a stronger 0.5 edge so they pop.
- **Edge lines** — in the wall pass, the topmost painted wall row of a
  column at 1.25× brightness (clamped) and the bottom row at 0.8×
  (contact shadow). A column whose hit cell or hit side differs from
  its left neighbour's (a corner or a different wall block) gets its
  whole wall span at 1.15× — vertical corner lines.
- Keep it fast: no allocations per pixel; the 200×60 timing test must
  stay under 8 ms average with detail on (log it).
