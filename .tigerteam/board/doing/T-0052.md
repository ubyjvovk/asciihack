## Goal
The browser has two views: first-person (`F2`) and an **orthographic** 3/4
overhead (`F3`). Afterburn's look is neither — it is a **perspective long-lens
follow camera** behind and above the subject, and that camera is a large part
of why its world reads as a photographed diorama. Add it as a third view so
the user can have the same look.

## Context
- **The sources are vendored in-repo at `vendor/afterburn/` (read-only).**
  `~/afterburn` is not visible inside the worker container. The camera rig is
  **not** vendored (it lives in their `src/game/camera.js`), so the numbers
  you need are quoted below from `vendor/afterburn/docs/ART_BIBLE.md` §6 and
  from the PM's reading of that rig.
- ART_BIBLE §6, verbatim: *"Perspective, FOV ≈ 28–32°, pitch ≈ 38–46°,
  distance 22–34 m, follows the player with a soft spring. Depth of field
  focused on the player, gentle (diorama, not mush)."* Their rig's defaults
  are `fov 30`, `pitch 42°`, `dist 26` (clamped 18–34), look-at height
  `0.95` above the subject's feet, yaw snapped to **45° steps**, springs on
  position/yaw/distance.
- **Scale conversion — this is the one thing that must not be copied
  literally.** Afterburn is metres with a ~1.7 m character; here one cell is
  one unit and the hero sprite is `HERO_SPRITE_HEIGHT = 0.7` cells. So
  distances scale by `0.7 / 1.7 ≈ 0.41`:
  | afterburn | here |
  |---|---|
  | dist 26 m (default) | **10.7 cells** |
  | dist 18 m (min) | 7.4 cells |
  | dist 34 m (max) | 14.0 cells |
  | look-at height 0.95 m | 0.39 cells |
  **FOV 30° and pitch 42° are angles — copy them exactly, do not scale.**
- **Implement:** `web/src/gpu/thirdperson.ts` with a pure
  `thirdPersonPose(hero, yawSteps, dist, opts)` returning
  `{ position, target, fov, focus }` — camera position from pitch/yaw/dist
  around a target at `(hero.x + 0.5, 0.39, hero.y + 0.5)`, and `focus` =
  the camera-to-target distance, so DOF lands on the hero exactly as §6 asks.
  Spring smoothing is a separate pure `dampPose(current, wanted, dt)`; keep
  both testable in node.
- `GlViewport` gains `'third'` alongside `'fps'` and `'ortho'`. It is a
  **perspective** camera, so unlike ortho it needs no SSGI demotion and no
  fog rescale — but do check the fog: at ~11 cells the mood densities give
  `e^(−0.10·11) ≈ 33 %` survival, which is atmospheric rather than black.
  Say what you measured in the docs; do not silently rescale it.
- Controls: **`F4`** switches to the third-person view (verify F4 is unbound
  in `src/ui/app.ts` and `web/src/main.ts` first — F1–F3, F5–F8 are taken; if
  F4 is taken, use F9 and say so). `Q`/`E` rotate the camera in 45° steps,
  the mouse wheel zooms between the min and max above. `?view=third` selects
  it, matching the existing `?view=fps|ortho`.
- `web/src/scene-bench.ts` is in scope **only** to accept `?view=third`
  alongside its existing `?view=ortho` — it is the PM's review instrument.
