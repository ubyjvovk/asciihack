## Goal
Movement is still jumpy after T-0060. The smoother works and is called every
frame — but its output only reaches the **camera and the lantern**. The
avatar is still drawn at its raw integer cell, so the model teleports a whole
cell on every NetHack turn while the camera glides after it. Give the hero
(and the pet) the smoothed position.

## Context
- `web/src/gl/gl-viewport.ts:510` computes
  `const smoothedPose = this.heroSmoother.update(heroCell, pose.yaw, dt)` and
  uses it for `this.camera.position` (518), the third-person spring (593) and
  the GPU render call (596).
- But `web/src/gpu/sprites.ts` places **every** sprite — avatars included —
  at `obj.position.set(s.x + 0.5, 0, s.y + 0.5)` (lines 128, 136, 158), where
  `s.x`/`s.y` are the integer cells from `spritesFromMap`. That is the jump.
- **The fix, decided.** `SpriteLayer.update` already takes an optional
  `pose`. Extend it to take the **smoothed hero world position** and use it
  for the hero sprite (`ch === '@'`) instead of its cell centre. The pet
  should glide too: it has no smoother of its own, so give `SpriteLayer` a
  small per-sprite ease — the same exponential damper, same
  `POSE_SNAP_CELLS` snap rule — applied to **every** sprite keyed by its
  sprite identity, so monsters glide as well. A monster that blinks a whole
  cell is the same defect.
  - Reuse the damper from `createPoseSmoother` rather than writing a second
    one; export what you need from `src/ui/view3d.ts` if it is not already.
  - **Snap, do not glide**, for a sprite that appears, disappears, or moves
    more than `POSE_SNAP_CELLS` (1.9) — a monster teleporting across the room
    must not skate.
- Billboards yaw toward the camera as they do now; only the position changes.
