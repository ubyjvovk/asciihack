## Goal
The user likes the amber look but finds it crude: wall-top edges are bold
white-yellow bands, the floor grid and flagstone seams turn into thick
`]]]]]` bands near the camera, the brick courses are noisy — and a known
corridor cell beyond a doorway renders exactly like unknown space, so two
walkable steps look like one. Refine all of it in the raycaster.

## Context
- `docs/render.md` (levels from T-0023/T-0029), `src/render/raycast.ts`
  (wall pass edge constants `EDGE_TOP/EDGE_BOT/EDGE_CORNER/EDGE_POST/
  EDGE_GRID`, floor pass with `gridShade`/`floorShade`, the doorway
  threshold frame), `src/render/texture.ts` (`brickShade`, `floorShade`,
  `gridShade`). Quantizer facts: black point 0.10 after exposure 1.7;
  the amber theme blooms to warm white above v ≈ 0.82, i.e. above 0.48
  linear — keep every line **below** that so lines stay amber, not
  white.
- **Edges thinner and softer.** Absolute levels: wall top edge `0.42`
  (was 0.75), bottom contact `0.20`, corner columns `0.34`, door posts
  `0.40`, stairs highlight `0.60` (the one thing allowed to bloom). The
  top edge is exactly one screen row per column (the first painted wall
  row), never two.
- **Screen-space seams.** Replace the world-space seam width for the
  floor grid and flagstone seams with edge detection in screen space: in
  the floor pass, a cell is a grid line if the floor cell index
  `(floor(fX), floor(fY))` differs from the sample directly above it (row
  − 1) or to its left (column − 1); flagstone seams likewise on the
  half-cell index. That makes every seam exactly one cell thick at any
  distance. Grid lines at `0.16` absolute, flagstone seams at `0.045`
  (a dark seam between stones, no longer a bright one), stone bodies
  ±15 %.
- **Calmer brick.** Brick body ±0.02, mortar `0.11` (so courses are a
  faint darker line, not a dotted contour); keep the seed rule.
- **Corridors readable.** `KIND_COLORS.corridor` `[0.12, 0.115, 0.11]`
  (was 0.07) with rough-rock stones of side 1.0 and no seams, so a known
  corridor cell quantizes to dots like a floor cell, distinct from the
  unknown veil which stays black. Check: facing along a doorway into a
  corridor 2 cells deep, the corridor row is above the black point at
  1 and 2 cells.
- **No floor threshold frame.** Remove the bright rectangle the floor
  pass draws around doorway thresholds; a doorway is the two vertical
  posts in the wall pass (now 0.40) and the door-coloured floor only.
- Regenerate both goldens; add/adjust tests (by name, only these):
  `top edge is one row and below the bloom level` (per column exactly
  one cell in the 0.35–0.48 band above the wall body), `grid seams are
  one cell thick at 1.5 cells distance` (no two vertically adjacent
  seam cells in a column of the near floor), `corridor cell beyond a
  doorway is visible` (row brightness above 0.06 at 2 cells), `doorway
  floor has no bright frame` (no floor cell in the doorway's rows above
  0.3 except the posts' columns), goldens match, timing under 8 ms.
- `docs/render.md`: levels table and the seam rule updated.
