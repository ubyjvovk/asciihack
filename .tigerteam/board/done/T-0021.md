## Goal

diamond in the middle of a black screen, reads as top-down rather than
3/4, and the hero is two cells tall. Rework `renderOrtho` so that the

- **Zoom.** `k = clamp(round(fb.height / 28), 1, 6)` unless
  `opts.zoom` is given. A map cell is a 2:1 diamond `4k` columns wide and
  `2k` rows tall. Wall height `h = 3k` rows. Figure height `3.5k` rows
  (so at 104 rows, k = 4: tiles 16×8, walls 12, hero 14 ≈ 1/7).
- **Projection (exact inverse, no ambiguity).** The screen position of a
  map point `(X, Y)` (continuous, x east, y south) is
  `c = ox + 2k·(X − Y)`, `r = oy + k·(X + Y)`. A screen cell `(c, r)` maps
  back through its centre: `u = (c + 0.5 − ox) / (2k)`,
  `v = (r + 0.5 − oy) / k`, `X = (u + v) / 2`, `Y = (v − u) / 2`, map cell
  `= (floor(X), floor(Y))`. Because tiles are the images of unit squares,
  every screen cell belongs to exactly one map cell. `cellToScreen(x, y)`
  returns the screen position of the cell centre `(x + 0.5, y + 0.5)`;
  `screenToCell(c, r)` is the inverse above. `(ox, oy)` place the hero's
  cell centre at `(floor(cols / 2), floor(rows · 0.55))` (a little below
  centre so walls above have room).
- **Floor pass** (every screen cell, one inverse mapping each): map cell
  kind → colour from `KIND_COLORS` with fog `exp(−0.04 · dist)` (Chebyshev
  distance from the hero); tile seams: cells whose `X` or `Y` fractional
  part is within `0.12 / k` of an edge are darkened ×0.6; a checker
  ×0.92 on `(x + y)` odd; corridors get no checker. `unexplored` cells:
  black `[0, 0, 0]` with the seam lines at `[0.05, 0.05, 0.07]` (the
  lattice), also outside the 80×21 map so the lattice covers the whole
  viewport. `doorway`/`door_open`: floor in the door colour.
- **Wall pass, painter's order** (increasing `x + y`, then `x`): for every
  cell with `isSolid(kind)` except `unexplored` (`stone` renders as a
  block in its flat grey; `door_closed` in the door colour): the block is
  the tile's diamond D (rows `sy − k … sy + k`, columns `sx − 2k … sx + 2k`)
  extruded `h` rows up. Paint, for each column `c` of D, the rows from
  `bottom(c) − h` to `bottom(c)` where `bottom(c)` is D's lower boundary
  row at that column: columns left of `sx` are the south-west face at
  ×0.55, right of `sx` the south-east face at ×0.75, both with
  `brickShade(u, v, seed)` (u along the face, v down it); then the top
  face = D shifted up by `h` at ×1.0 with a 1-cell lighter rim on its
  upper two edges (×1.2) and a 1-cell dark line on its lower two edges
  (×0.6); the vertical corner at `sx` between the two faces ×1.1. Every
  painted cell writes depth and clears `overlayCh`.
- **Sprites** (drawn with their tile in the painter step so nearer walls
  cover them): monsters (`cls` mon/pet/ridden/detected/invisible/statue)
  and the hero: a figure of width `2k + 1` (odd) and height `3.5k`
  standing with its feet at the tile centre row `sy` (so it overlaps the
  tile behind, which is correct for 3/4). Ellipse mask, radial brightness
  `0.55 + 0.45·(1 − r²)`, rim ring at 0.22, letter everywhere (as
  T-0017's raycaster figures). Items (`cls` obj/body): `2k + 1` wide,
  `1.5k` tall, centred on the tile. Far/small (k = 1): one or two cells,
  no rim.
- Goldens: regenerate `tests/ortho-golden.txt` (80×24 → k = 1, ROOM, hero
  centre) and add `tests/ortho-zoom-golden.txt` (120×56 → k = 2, ROOM
  with the hero, one monster and one item) with the test-local ramp.
- Performance: 200×60 (k = 2) under 8 ms average; the floor pass is one
  inverse mapping per screen cell, the wall pass touches only cells
  near the viewport.

1. **Spec deviation on figure width.** Context says figure width `2k + 1`; the
   acceptance requires the hero at k = 4 to be **7 wide** (not 9). I implemented

   threshold the lattice is provably invisible: cell-centre inverse samples

   is exactly 1/(8k) in every case). I widened the band to `0.2/k` so the
   lattice reliably shows, satisfying the "player never floats in pure black"
   goal. Noted in `docs/render.md`.
