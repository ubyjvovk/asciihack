## Goal
`createLook()` carries afterburn's outdoor DOF defaults — `focus: 30`,
`focusRange: 38`, `bokeh: 1.0` metres — and **no dungeon mood overrides
them**. One cell is one metre here, so the whole dungeon lives inside 10 m
and everything renders permanently defocused. Give every mood a focus
distance that belongs indoors.

## Context
- **The sources are vendored in-repo at `vendor/afterburn/` (read-only).**
  `~/afterburn` is not visible inside the worker container.
- Read `docs/gpu.md` §4 (one cell = one three.js unit = one "metre") and
  `docs/gpu-materials.md` (the mood table). The `Mood.look` records currently
  set `exposure`, `contrast`, `saturation`, `tintAmount`, `vignette`, `grain`,
  `bloomStrength`, `ssrIntensity` and `giIntensity` — `focus`, `focusRange`
  and `bokeh` are simply absent, so the pipeline defaults win.
- **The values, decided** — add these three keys to **every** mood in `MOODS`:
  `focus: 5`, `focusRange: 9`, `bokeh: 0.7`. Five cells ahead is roughly
  where the eye goes in a corridor, a nine-cell range keeps the near floor
  and the far wall both acceptably sharp, and 0.7 takes the edge off the
  bokeh so the blur does not smear torch highlights into discs.
  Two exceptions: `flooded` takes `bokeh: 0.9` (the reflections are the
  subject and a little more roll-off flatters them), and `deep_dark` takes
  `focus: 3.5` (you cannot see five cells anyway).
- This is a three-key-per-record edit. Do not change any other number, do not
  touch the pipeline, and do not "improve" the DOF maths.
