## Goal
Three small level tweaks that go with the quantizer tone-curve change
(T-0028): the unknown veil must be neutral (its blue cast became a blue
wall), its speckle must be sparser and dimmer, and the corridor floor
must be neutral grey (its warm cast became yellow).

## Context
- `src/render/raycast.ts` `KIND_COLORS`: `unexplored` → `[0.03, 0.03, 0.03]`
  (was `…0.05`), `stone` → `[0.12, 0.12, 0.12]`, `corridor` →
  `[0.07, 0.07, 0.07]` (was warm). Doorway/door tints stay warm — they
  are meant to read as wood.
- `src/render/texture.ts` `veilShade`: about 8 % of samples non-zero
  (was ~12 %), values in `0.05–0.10` (was 0.10–0.22) — under the new
  black point they come out as rare `.` glyphs.
- **Corner lines fire on every cell boundary.** T-0023's rule brightens a
  column whenever its hit *cell* differs from the left neighbour's, so a
  flat wall three cells away shows a bright vertical at each cell seam
  (the PM's capture looks like a row of pillars). A corner is only where
  the hit **side** changes (N/S face ↔ E/W face) or the perpendicular
  distance jumps by more than 0.5 cells between adjacent columns. Fix
  the condition; brick seams stay the dark mortar lines.
- **Wall bodies vanish under the new black point.** With `fogK 0.28` a
  wall body (0.14) three cells away is `0.14·e^(−0.84) ≈ 0.06` → below
  the quantizer's black point (0.10 after exposure), so walls render as
  edge lines only and look like the unknown. Change: `fogK` default
  `0.14`; `KIND_COLORS.wall` body `[0.18, 0.18, 0.19]` (door/tree/bars
  bases +0.04 likewise); absolute edge/grid lines get fog at half
  strength (`· e^(−0.07·d)`) so they fade instead of glowing at any
  distance. Check the numbers: body at 3 cells ≈ 0.12 (sparse glyphs),
  at 6 ≈ 0.08 (faint), at 10 → space; top edge at 10 cells ≈ 0.37
  (still a line).
- Regenerate the textured golden; update the veil/stone/corridor colour
  expectations in the tests and the colour table in `docs/render.md`.
