## Goal
T-0048 added `moodEnvironment()` to `moods.ts` and an `environment` option to
`createPipeline`, but the wiring was out of its scope, so **nothing passes
one** and every frame on the WebGL2 backend still logs
`TypeError: Cannot read properties of null (reading 'sampleEnvironmentBRDF')`.
One argument and one mood-change hook close it.

## Context
- **The sources are vendored in-repo at `vendor/afterburn/` (read-only).**
- Read `docs/gpu-pipeline.md` (the `environment` option), `docs/gpu-materials.md`
  (`moodEnvironment`, `Atmosphere.environment`) and `docs/gpu-compose.md`
  (how `GpuPath` builds the pipeline today).
- **Two changes in `web/src/gpu/path.ts`, and nothing else:**
  1. `GpuPath.create` sets the atmosphere's mood **before** building the
     pipeline, then passes `environment: atmosphere.environment` into
     `createPipeline`.
  2. `SSRNode` reads `environmentNode` at construction, so a later mood change
     does not reach it. After `Atmosphere` swaps moods, call
     `state.nodes['ssr']?.setEnvMap?.(atmosphere.environment)` — guarded, since
     `nodes.ssr` is absent on the `low` tier and `setEnvMap` may not exist on
     every three version. Never throw from the render loop because of it.
- Do **not** touch `moods.ts`, `pipeline.ts` or `dungeon.ts`.

### For the reviewer to look at closely
- The `Object.assign(lookProxy, handle.look)` pattern for closing the
  atmosphere/pipeline chicken-and-egg. It is intentional and commented; the

- The `state.nodes['ssr']?.setEnvMap?.(...)` cast in `render()` — kept
  minimal (a single `as { setEnvMap?: ... } | undefined`) and wrapped in
  `try/catch` so the render loop is never taken down by a missing method
  or a three r185-level change to `SSRNode`.

  a non-torchlit tile (e.g. `flooded`): I traced it — `atmosphere.set` in

  one; `setEnvMap` runs synchronously right after (still in the same
  frame's `render()` call) so SSR never renders against a disposed
  texture. Frames don't render between those two lines.
